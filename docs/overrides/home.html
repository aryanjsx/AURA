Free and open source · Windows, macOS, Linux

An AI assistant that runs on your computer, not someone else's.

Locus runs language models on your own machine. Ask it questions, have it read your notes and documents, or set it to send you a briefing every morning. Nothing leaves your computer unless you connect a service yourself.

Free · no account needed

What you can use it for

Locus is one app with a few jobs. Each works with a model on your machine; cloud models are there only if you add your own API key.

Ask and write

  • explain this error message
  • rewrite this email so it's shorter
  • what's the difference between TCP and UDP

Type or talk to it. It can read answers aloud.

Your files and notes

  • find the PDF about my car insurance
  • summarize the meeting notes from last week
  • what did I decide about the kitchen paint

Indexes folders, PDFs, Obsidian, Notion, Google Drive and more, on your machine.

Things on a schedule

  • a morning briefing from email, calendar and news
  • a research report with sources, while you're away
  • a check on a web page every hour

Get the results in the app, or on Telegram, Slack, Discord or email.

Install it like any other app

You don't need Python, Ollama or any developer tools. Locus sets itself up the first time it opens.

  1. 1

    Download and open it

    One installer for your system. The download button above picks the right one.

  2. 2

    Choose where models run

    Pick Ollama to run them on this computer, or point Locus at a server you already use, like LM Studio.

  3. 3

    Wait a few minutes, once

    Locus installs what it needs and downloads a model that fits your memory. After that it starts in seconds.

Private because it's local

Most assistants are a chat box connected to a company's servers. Locus keeps the model, your documents and your history on your own disk.

Runs on your machine
Models run through Ollama or another local engine. Cloud models are opt-in.
No analytics
Locus ships with no analytics destination, so nothing about your use is sent anywhere.
You choose what it can see
Email, calendar and chat apps are off until you connect them, one by one.
It asks before risky actions
Running shell commands and similar tools needs your approval first.

Before you install

The installers aren't code-signed yet, so your system will warn you the first time. On Windows, choose More info → Run anyway. On a Mac, right-click Locus and choose Open.

8 GB of memory is enough for a small model; 16 GB runs noticeably better ones. A graphics card helps but isn't required.

Prefer the command line?

macOS, Linux and WSL2:

curl -fsSL https://aryanjsx.github.io/Locus/install.sh | bash

Windows PowerShell:

irm https://aryanjsx.github.io/Locus/install.ps1 | iex

Or pip install locus-assistant. See the installation guide.
